Terms & Conditions

FIX MY WORK is a platform that helps customers connect with service professionals.

1. Service Requests

Customers must provide accurate service, contact and location information.

2. Professional Acceptance

A service request remains in searching status until an eligible professional accepts it.

3. Cancellation

A customer or professional may cancel a request according to the applicable platform rules.

4. Customer Responsibility

Customers must provide safe access to the service location and accurate information.

5. Safety

Never share passwords, OTPs, PINs or confidential financial information with another person.

6. Reviews

Customers may submit honest ratings and feedback after a completed service.

7. Platform Role

FIX MY WORK facilitates the connection between customers and professionals. Specific service arrangements remain between the parties unless otherwise stated by the platform.
